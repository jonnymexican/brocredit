FriendCredit™ Privacy Policy

Bureau of Friend Conduct — Records Division

The short version

FriendCredit keeps all data on your own device. There is no server, no account system, and no analytics. When you link Facebook, we receive only your public profile name and profile photo, and they never leave your device.

What we collect

  • Your friend ledger — citizens you add and the points you file — stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Facebook public profile (optional) — if you connect Facebook, the app requests the public_profile permission only, receiving your display name and small profile photo. Nothing else: no email, no friend list, no posts, no messages.

How your Facebook data is handled

  • Your name and photo are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device.
  • The only network calls the app makes related to Facebook are the login dialog at facebook.com and the profile lookup at graph.facebook.com.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data. We have no server to store it on.
  • Linking Facebook is entirely optional — every feature works without it.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Bureau Stats → Facebook liaison → Unlink removes the stored profile immediately. "Dissolve the Bureau" wipes the whole ledger.
  • On Facebook: removing the app at Settings → Apps and Websites revokes access.
  • See Data Deletion Instructions for the full walkthrough, or email jonathan_voigt@hotmail.com.
