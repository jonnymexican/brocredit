Data Deletion Instructions

Bureau of Friend Conduct — Records Incineration

Good news first

FriendCredit stores everything only on your own device — there is no server copy of your data. Deleting it locally is complete deletion. The only data ever received from Facebook is your public profile name and photo.

Option 1 — Delete everything in the app

  1. Open FriendCredit.
  2. Facebook profile: Bureau Stats → Facebook liaison → Unlink.
  3. Entire ledger: Bureau Stats → Regime change → Dissolve the Bureau.
  4. Or clear the site's data in your browser settings (Site settings → Clear data), which removes every stored record at once.

Option 2 — Remove the app via Facebook

  1. Go to Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find FriendCredit Login and select Remove.
  3. This revokes the app's access immediately. Because we store nothing off-device, no further server-side deletion is needed.

Option 3 — Ask the Bureau in writing

Email jonathan_voigt@hotmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". Since no data is held on any server, you will receive confirmation that nothing outside your device exists to delete, plus help clearing your device if you want it.

The records were never here.
